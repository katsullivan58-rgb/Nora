1. Introduction

NORA ("Navigating Obstetric Resources & Access") is an AI-powered maternal health companion designed to support individuals through pregnancy, postpartum, and family planning journeys. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data.

By using NORA, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please discontinue use of the service.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only the information necessary to personalize your NORA experience:

  • Journey information: Your current stage (trying to conceive, pregnant, postpartum), pregnancy week, due date, and last menstrual period (LMP).
  • Location: ZIP code (used to surface relevant local resources).
  • Contact information: Phone number, if you opt in to receive a one-time SMS text of NORA's response.
  • Conversation data: Messages you send to NORA to generate responses and improve the service.
  • Anonymous identifier: A randomly generated ID stored in your browser to maintain session continuity without requiring account creation.

We do not collect your full name, date of birth, insurance information, or medical records unless you explicitly provide them in conversation.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used solely to:

  • Personalize NORA's responses to your current journey stage and pregnancy week.
  • Surface relevant local health resources based on your ZIP code.
  • Send a one-time SMS message containing NORA's response, if you have opted in and requested it.
  • Improve the accuracy and quality of NORA's guidance.
  • Maintain session continuity across visits.
NORA is an informational tool, not a medical provider. Information you share is used to generate supportive, resource-based responses — not for clinical diagnosis, treatment, or medical recordkeeping.

4. Information We Do Not Share

We are committed to protecting your privacy. We will never:

  • Sell your personal information to third parties.
  • Share your data with advertisers or marketing companies.
  • Use your information for purposes unrelated to your NORA experience.
  • Disclose your health-related information to employers, insurers, or government entities except as required by law.

We may share data with trusted service providers (such as our hosting provider, Supabase) who process data strictly on our behalf and are bound by confidentiality obligations. These providers do not use your data for their own purposes.

5. SMS Communications

If you opt in after receiving a response from NORA, you consent to receive a one-time text message containing that specific response. Messages are not recurring or scheduled, and are sent only when you request one.

  • Message and data rates may apply depending on your mobile carrier plan.
  • One message is sent per opt-in request. There is no ongoing or scheduled messaging.
  • Reply STOP at any time to unsubscribe from all SMS messages.
  • Reply HELP for assistance or contact us at the address below.
  • Your phone number will never be shared with third parties for marketing purposes.

6. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored securely using Supabase, a SOC 2 Type II compliant database platform. We implement industry-standard security practices including:

  • Encrypted data transmission (TLS/HTTPS).
  • Row-level security policies restricting access to your data.
  • Access controls limiting which personnel can access user data.

While we take reasonable measures to protect your information, no system is completely secure. We encourage you not to share highly sensitive medical or financial information through the chat interface.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of the data we hold about you.
  • Deletion: Request that your data be deleted from our systems.
  • Correction: Request corrections to inaccurate information.
  • Opt-out: Withdraw consent for SMS communications at any time by replying STOP.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the information in Section 10.

8. Minors

NORA is intended for individuals 18 years of age and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors. If you believe a minor has provided information to NORA, please contact us immediately and we will delete that information.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Effective Date" at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically. Continued use of NORA after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
