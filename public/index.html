Where NORA comes from

A name carries
a history.

NORA is named for Ellenora, "Nora", a real woman, a great grandmother, who endured the violence of the eugenics era. Her choices were taken. Her voice was silenced. Her body was not her own.

These histories are not distant. They shape how care is experienced across generations. NORA was built so that no woman has to face that system alone again.

"Black women reclaiming the technologies that have so often left us behind, to build something better, creating not only change for ourselves but expanding what care can look like so that all women are seen, supported, and truly cared for."

— Jordan Williams, Founder of NORA
Ellenora's family, across generations
The legacy she left

Every woman in this photo
is here because she persisted.

Ellenora survived. She built a family. Generations of women exist because of her strength. NORA exists to protect every generation that comes next, so that no woman's story ends before it should.

Three generations. One name. One mission.

How NORA works

Simple. Personal.
Always there.

01
Tell NORA your story

Set up your profile in under a minute, your journey stage, how far along you are, and where you're located. NORA uses this to personalize everything.

02
Ask anything, anytime

Nutrition questions. Symptom concerns. Baby development. Birth prep. Warm, evidence-based guidance 24/7, no appointment needed.

03
Get connected to care

NORA connects you to local clinics, WIC offices, birth centers, and maternal health programs, all tailored to your location and needs.

04
Never walk in alone

NORA helps you prepare for appointments, understand your options, and advocate for yourself, because every woman deserves to be heard in the room.
